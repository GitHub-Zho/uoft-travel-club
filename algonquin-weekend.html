Campground comfort

Comfortable campground setup

This is a designated, drive-in public campground—not remote backcountry camping. Shared drinking water, hot showers, flush toilets, and laundry facilities are available. More than half of the campsites have electrical hookups; the final site assignment will confirm whether yours does.

You’ll still sleep in a tent and bring your own sleeping gear.

  • Drinking water
  • Hot showers
  • Flush toilets
  • Laundry facilities
Trip details

What kind of trip is this?

New campers are welcome

This trip is beginner-friendly. You do not need prior camping experience, but you should be comfortable sleeping outdoors for two nights and following group safety instructions.

One person, one tent — planned

Our working plan is one participant per tent. You may instead choose to share a tent with a friend named on the form; we will then recalculate the campsite portion you share and confirm your final total. Ontario Parks allows a maximum of six people and three tents per campsite, so we will reserve enough campsites for the final tent count.

Equipment

What is there, and what do I bring?

Lake of Two Rivers provides

  • A designated campsite and level tent area
  • A picnic table and fire pit with a cooking grill
  • Shared comfort stations with showers and flush toilets
  • Central drinking-water taps and campsite parking

You should plan to bring

  • Your own tent, sleeping bag, and sleeping pad
  • Warm layers, rain protection, and suitable footwear
  • A headlamp or flashlight
  • Your own bowl, cup, and cutlery

We coordinate as a group

Shared pots, pans, camp stoves, coolers, water containers, and safety supplies will be coordinated by the club. You do not need to bring a full cooking set unless the organizers assign a shared item to you.

Missing something?

For this trip, participants could use the UTOC Gear Library to check the available tent, sleeping bag, sleeping pad, and other equipment, then book the rental yourself through that page. UTOC rentals are separate from the trip price and first-come, first-served.

Roofed accommodation requests

A yurt or cabin at nearby Mew Lake was an optional request, not a confirmed booking. If UTETC could not offer the requested accommodation and a participant did not accept an available tent-camping place, the registration deposit will be refunded.
