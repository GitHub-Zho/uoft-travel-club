Before you register

What kind of trip is this?

New campers are welcome

This trip is beginner-friendly. You do not need prior camping experience, but you should be comfortable sleeping outdoors for two nights and following group safety instructions.

One person, one tent — planned

Our working plan is one participant per tent. Ontario Parks allows a maximum of six people and three tents per campsite, so we will reserve enough campsites for the final tent count.

Equipment

What is there, and what do I bring?

Mew Lake provides

  • A designated campsite and level tent area
  • A picnic table and fire pit with a cooking grill
  • Shared comfort stations with showers and flush toilets
  • Central drinking-water taps and campsite parking

You should plan to bring

  • Your own tent, sleeping bag, and sleeping pad
  • Warm layers, rain protection, and suitable footwear
  • A headlamp or flashlight
  • Your own bowl, cup, and cutlery

We coordinate as a group

Shared pots, pans, camp stoves, coolers, water containers, and safety supplies will be coordinated by the club. You do not need to bring a full cooking set unless the organizers assign a shared item to you.

Missing something?

You can still register. Tell us whether you need a tent, sleeping bag, sleeping pad, or another item, and we can help arrange a rental. Personal rental charges are separate from the trip price.
